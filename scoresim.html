GuideHow to use it

Where do I find the odds?

Open the match at any bookmaker or odds site. You need two markets:

  • 1X2 (also called Match Result or Full Time Result): three prices for home win, draw, away win.
  • Total Goals Over/Under 2.5: two prices.

Use decimal odds like 2.10. The tool removes the bookmaker margin for you, so any bookmaker works. Without Over/Under it still works, just less precisely.

What does the simulation do?

From the odds, the tool works out how many goals each team should score on average. Then it plays the match again and again with random goals and counts how often each score comes up. More simulations give steadier numbers. 100 jumps around, 10,000 is steady.

How do I read the score grid?

Rows are home goals, columns are away goals. The brighter the cell, the more often that score came up. The cell with the amber border is the most common score.

What are fair odds?

Fair odds are the price with no bookmaker margin: 1 divided by the chance. A score that comes up 12.5% of the time has fair odds of 8.00. If a bookmaker offers clearly less than the fair odds, the price is poor.

Why is 1:1 on top so often?

In close matches, 1:1 is often the single most common score, even when one team is a bit stronger. Bookmakers price it the same way. It still misses almost 9 times out of 10. Correct scores are hard.

Can I use correct score odds instead?

Yes. Open "Have correct score odds?" in a match and paste them as home:away odds, for example 1:0 8.5, 2:0 6.5, 1:1 17. With 10 or more scores, the simulator uses them directly. Tip: in the away team column of many bookmakers, 1-0 means an away win, so type it as 0:1.

Are my matches saved?

Yes, on this device and in this browser only. Nobody else sees them.

Is this a betting tip?

No. It shows what the odds already say, in a way you can see. It cannot beat the bookmaker. Use it to understand scores, for prediction games, or just for fun.
